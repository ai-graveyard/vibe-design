## Recommendation

Offer up to three candidates with a reason tied to the user's actual task and a concrete tradeoff. For example, a dense data view needs explicit field hierarchy and stable states; a material effect may be limited to navigation while the table uses solid surfaces. Explain the combination and preserve the existing brand.
